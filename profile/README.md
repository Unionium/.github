# Unionium

## About

Unionium is an organization that takes familiar things and builds them anew: simpler, faster, more reliable. Not for the sake of reinvention itself, but for a result that's measurably better than what came before.

## Principles

- **Rethink, don't copy.** We take a problem and look for a solution from scratch, without clinging to "that's how it's always been done."
- **Improvement is the bar.** If the new thing isn't better than the old one, we don't need it.
- **Simplicity as a goal.** The best solution is the one that's clear and free of excess.
- **Openness.** We share what we build and learn from those who build. Our projects are typically licensed under **[GPL](LICENSE)**

## What we do

We develop tools, services, and approaches that replace the outdated or the inconvenient. From small utilities to entire concepts.
